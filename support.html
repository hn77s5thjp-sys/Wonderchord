How can we help?

Email connorclements@hotmail.com. Common questions:

No sound?

Turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open, and check Bluetooth isn't sending sound to a speaker in another room. Strum Zoo plays even with the silent switch on — it's an instrument.

How do songs work?

Tap 🎵 and pick a song. Turn on Wait for me! in that same menu to make every song a lesson: it pauses at each chord and the right pad glows until your child presses it. Finishing a song that way earns a ⭐️.

Where's the parent dashboard?

Hold the 👪 button for two seconds (a quick tap just shows a hint — that's the child-lock). You'll see total stars and what each song teaches.

How do I stop the held chord?

Press the lit pad again — it toggles off. Press any pad to start again.

Turtle and bunny?

Drum speeds: 🐢 slow, 🐰 fast.

Does it need internet? Are there ads?

No and no. Everything works offline, forever. No ads, no purchases, no data collected — see our privacy promise.

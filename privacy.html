Privacy

Effective RELEASE DATE · This policy is written to be read by parents, not lawyers.

Wonderchord collects no data. From anyone. Especially not from children.

No analytics, no advertising, no accounts, no tracking, no third-party services, and the app makes no network connections at all.

Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Wonderchord is designed for children under 13 and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and equivalent laws by the simplest possible means: it collects nothing, transmits nothing, and contains no ads, no purchases, no social features and no external links. There is nothing for a child to share and nothing for us to receive.

Progress & stars

Stars and song progress shown in the Grown-Ups corner are stored only on your device. Delete the app and they're gone. They are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests none: no microphone, no camera, no photos, no location, no contacts.

Purchases

Wonderchord is bought once through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no personal or payment information. There are no in-app purchases of any kind.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated first and the change described plainly in the App Store notes.
